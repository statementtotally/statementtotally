How to import the XML into Tally

Works with TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9. Takes about a minute.
  1. Convert your statement here. Pick your bank, drag the PDF into the press, then fill the Company Name and Bank Ledger Name exactly as they appear in Tally. Download the XML.
  2. Open the same company in Tally. The company that is open must have the same name you typed in the tool.
  3. TallyPrime: press Alt + O (Import) → Transactions.
    ERP 9: Gateway of Tally → Import Data → Vouchers.
  4. Set File Path to the folder where the XML is saved and choose the downloaded file. Keep Behaviour of import on Add (not Modify), then press Enter to import.
  5. Check the result. Open Day Book and the bank ledger and compare the closing balance with your bank statement. Tally also shows how many vouchers were created and any errors.
If import shows errors: the usual cause is a company name that doesn't match exactly (spelling, spaces, capital letters). Fix the name in the tool and download again. Imported the wrong file by mistake? Delete those vouchers in Tally first, then import the corrected one so entries don't double up.
Is my bank statement uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is read inside your browser and the XML is generated on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server. The only exception is the Need help message: it opens WhatsApp with your bank name and the problem you typed - your statement is never included.

Why does it ask for a Suspense ledger?

Every transaction is posted between your bank ledger and one ledger of your choice (Suspense Account by default). You can later reclassify entries in Tally.

Does it work for scanned or photo PDFs?

No. It needs a normal bank-generated PDF where you can select the text.

My bank's PDF doesn't convert. What now?

Tap Need help? and tell us the bank name. We add support for new formats regularly.

Is StatementToTally really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no payment and nothing to install. You open the page, pick your bank, drop in the PDF and download the XML.

Which banks does it support?

ICICI, HDFC, SBI, Axis, Canara, IDFC FIRST, Kotak Mahindra, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, Yes Bank, IndusInd, Union Bank, IDBI, Federal Bank, RBL and UCO Bank. Choose yours in Step 1. If your bank is not listed, try “Other / Not listed”.

Does it work with TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9?

Yes. The XML imports into both. The exact import steps for each are in the guide above.

Can I convert a password-protected bank statement PDF?

Yes. If the PDF is locked the tool asks for the password. It is used only inside your browser and is never sent anywhere.

I already entered some transactions in Tally. Can I skip them?

Yes. Use the “Already entered in Tally?” box: type the date and amount of the last entry you posted and the tool exports only the rows after it.

StatementToTally turns a bank statement PDF into an XML file that TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9 can import as vouchers, so you do not have to type each entry by hand. It is built for accountants, CAs and small businesses who reconcile bank statements every month.
